About

Different perspectives.
One practical approach.

My professional background spans audit, internal controls and regulation. These perspectives inform how I assess risk, question assumptions and consider whether governance arrangements work in practice.

I’m interested in the connection between sound professional judgment and useful technology: tools that help people navigate information, see its limits and make better-informed decisions.

This portfolio brings that interest to life through independent projects, with an emphasis on clarity, careful design and transparent sources.

Areas of focus

Audit & controls

Understanding risks, assessing control design and effectiveness, and communicating findings clearly.

Risk & governance

Connecting oversight, risk ownership and decision-making with how an organisation operates.

Regulation & insight

Making complex requirements easier to navigate while keeping sources, scope and uncertainty visible.
